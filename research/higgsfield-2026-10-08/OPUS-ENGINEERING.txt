2. PROPOSED FINISHING (no ping-pong, no slow-down, no still-to-still opacity)
=====================================================================================================
Generate ONE continuous take that moves from frame 0 to the end (the Greca pilot does), then cut it:

    reveal = take[0, a)          loop = take[a, b)          (b ≤ last frame)

* The reveal→loop handoff is the take's OWN continuity (frame a−1 → a): never a seam.
* The only artificial transition is the loop seam b−1 → a, repeated by AVPlayerLooper.
* (a, b) is chosen by measurement, not by eye-balling a timeline: the seam should look like one more
  frame of the take's own motion (magnitude, direction, speed, exposure, product, and no local pop).
* Works with the existing player unchanged (two files, same proportions; posters cut from the same
  frames: start = take frame 0, final/Reduce Motion = loop frame 0).

Order of preference when no interval passes:
  1. another generative take (8–10 s, steady-state motion, see §6);
  2. a generated BRIDGE: Kling start_image = frame b−1, end_image = frame a, 1–2 s; loop = take[a,b) +
     bridge. Both seams then coincide with real frames. Risk (documented by the v1 loops): anchored
     ends slow down/settle — the bridge must pass the same gates (tail/head live share);
  3. last resort: short crossfade of MOVING footage (--blend K): measured and reported, see §4.

3. IMPLEMENTATION (research/higgsfield-2026-10-08/tools/)

loop_interval.py — reuses collection-motion-2026-10-07/motion_regions.py (imported, not forked):
same hero-scale decode (360 px, visible 1:1.30 aspect-fill crop), blur, lag/window timeline, region
syntax and gates.
  scan  every admissible (a, b) (defaults: reveal 1.5–4 s, loop 3–6 s) on the phone crop:
        jump        mean |F[a]−F[b]| in the moving area / median step (F[b] = natural successor of b−1)
        local       the same for the worst 8×6 tile (catches a wave breaking in one place)
        seam_step   displayed step |F[a]−F[b−1]| / median step (≈1 natural, ≫1 pop, ≪1 hitch)
        direction   corr(F[a]−F[b−1], F[b]−F[b−1]) — motion keeps its direction (> 0)
        speed       0.25 s lagged change after a / before b (≈1: no speed jump)
        exposure    |mean luma F[a] − F[b]|
        rigid       |F[a]−F[b]| in rigid (product) regions
        + per-take: live share per 0.5 s window (ease-out/freeze detector), product drift vs frame 0,
          EDGE drift (high-pass) in rigid regions — separates light/caustics from geometry change.
        Top candidates are re-gated with motion_regions' own loop×3 measurement.
  cut   frame-exact reveal/loop from the full-res YUV (no RGB round trip), posters, manifest JSON
        (source sha256, job id, interval, seam metrics, encode, analyzer audit), heatmap; re-runs
        motion_regions.py on the ENCODED files. Options: --codec h264|hevc (hvc1, closed GOP 24),
        --crf, --blend K (reported doubling metric).
  Normalisation floor: median step ≥ 0.25 luma, so a frozen take's codec noise is not called a "jump".
selftest.py — synthetic takes, no generated media: 10/10 PASS
        periodic water over a still product → finds the 96-frame period, jump 0.011, seam ×1.009,
        direction 1.0, speed 1.0; cut writes exactly a and b−a frames; encoded re-audit PASS;
        drifting take → larger jump (gate, not silent pass); eased-out take holding a still for 1.5 s
        → last window dead; --blend reports HF dip 0.69.

5. ACCEPTANCE GATES (per film; numbers are heuristics, the final gate is Codex on device, 3 cycles)
=====================================================================================================
  take       live share ≥ 18 % in every 0.5 s window, median ≥ 30 %, last 1.5 s ≥ 0.6× median (no ease-out)
  product    rigid lagged ≤ 1.0 for geometry regions (rim/silhouette); edge drift ≤ 1.5× a still
             reference region (cliff/wall); lit areas (caustics) reported, judged visually
  seam       jump ≤ 1.5, local ≤ 3.0, displayed step 0.5–2.0×, direction ≥ 0, speed 0.67–1.5,
             exposure ≤ 0.8 luma
  handoff    reveal = take[0,a) → automatically natural (measured ×1.03 here)
  blend      only if no interval passes AND another take is not available: K ≤ 12 frames, HF ratio
             ≥ 0.85 in moving areas, product pair MAE ≤ 1.0, explicitly reported in the manifest
  files      reveal/loop same size, posters from the same frames, silent, closed GOP, ≤ ~6 MB per loop
  Greca pilot today: passes take/product/handoff; fails the seam gate (×2.69) and the local-pop review.

=====================================================================================================
6. RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE NEXT TAKES (reasoning, trade-offs)
=====================================================================================================
* Ask for STEADY STATE, not events: "uniform small ripples everywhere, no breaking waves, no foam, no
  water over the stone, nothing begins or ends". Events (foam sets, curtain gusts, leaf falls) are what
  make seams visible; uniform micro-motion hides them. Trade-off: less drama per frame.
* Prefer slower, finer water (smaller median step): the seam penalty is ~2.3 steps; a smaller step makes
  the same jump less visible. The pilot moves fast (median step 12 luma at hero scale).
* Longer take (10 s) gives more (a, b) pairs and a longer loop (6–7 s) that hides repetition.
* For Ninfea/Aria/Opera use the same pattern: a steady-state living take from the accepted final frame;
  growth/opening belongs to the reveal only if a separate steady take continues from its exact last
  frame (then the handoff is again a cut between takes and needs the same seam gates).
* Bridge generation (start = b−1, end = a) is the most promising way to an exact seam without blending;
  it costs one short generation per film and must pass the head/tail live-share gates.
