# Collection films — 6 October 2026

User scope confirmed: Aria, Opera and Greca on Home, plus a living loop after the existing Ninfea reveal. No automatic page changes or video controls. The reveal restarts on each new visit; the completed environment stays alive in a separate seamless ambient film.

Final direction, developed with Opus 5.5 (see OPUS-CONCEPTS.md):

- **Aria — breath / air.** The actual white bath with its distinctive suspended curved body and black side supports, in a tall limestone pavilion. Cool luminous sky, long translucent ivory curtains behind the bath. A slow gust opens the veils and reveals the space; then only fine fabric movement, water reflections and distant air remain. No camera travel or deformation of the bath.
- **Opera — light / time.** Preserve the stepped classical ivory silhouette. A sculptural travertine loggia with deep oxblood textile and warm bronze details. A controlled golden light sweep reveals the mouldings and settles into late-afternoon light. The ending retains tiny water ripples, distant water and barely moving fabric. No glowing particles or theatrical sparkles.
- **Greca — stone / tide.** Preserve the turquoise bowl, white lip and low ivory plinth. A contemporary mineral gallery facing an Aegean inlet, monumental limestone and dark stone. A shallow tide develops in a recessed channel around the dry product dais, reflecting light on the stone. The completed scene retains minute ripples and coherent moving caustics. The platform and bath remain rigid and dry outside.
- **Ninfea — mature garden.** Use the actual final decoded frame of the existing film as both endpoints of a four-second generated loop. No further growth or filling. Only very small leaf movement, stem flex and water shimmer.
