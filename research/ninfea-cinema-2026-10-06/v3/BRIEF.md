# Ninfea V3 — one living scene

6 October 2026. User rejected V2's sequential reveals, stretched cutouts, single vine, disconnected lighting and water appearing on the floor.

Create one continuous premium product shot with a locked camera and an unchanged real Salini Ninfea silhouette. Over approximately ten seconds, clear water rises inside the bath while multiple stems and leaves grow around and over its wings, simultaneously. Organic growth, moving leaf shadows, reflections and material response must be generated as one coherent scene. Dry limestone floor stays dry. No flood, opaque caustic wipe, cutout scaling, crossfade or moving background plate. End on a finished garden with the product still readable.

Use real video generation via Kling 3.0 Pro in the installed, authenticated Diffusion Studio, with approved model capabilities for start/end frames. Initial and final anchor frames are created with built-in imagegen, using the existing interior and original Salini product photograph as references. No credits are bought. Keep generations in this new project; do not modify the existing BalanceOS project.

In the iOS app, the artwork is ambient: no play, pause, replay, timecode, chapter bar or scrubber. Each new visit starts at the beginning, plays once, then holds the garden. Preserve the video's aspect ratio with a crop; no animated scaling. Reduce Motion uses the finished still. Collection information remains accessible as product information, not player chrome.

Acceptance: (1) unchanged tub shape and fixed viewpoint, (2) water and growth overlap from the beginning, (3) multiple integrated climbing stems and leaves, (4) consistent illumination and contact shadows, (5) no floor-water overlay, (6) no visible UI playback controls, (7) automatic restart on return and final hold. Inspect the actual generated video at early, middle and late timestamps and the app in Simulator; do not call a poor generation production-ready.
